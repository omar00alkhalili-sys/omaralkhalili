<h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
<p class="updated">LUXE DASH — Car Launcher &nbsp;·&nbsp; Last updated: 13 August 2026</p>

<p>This Privacy Policy explains how LUXE DASH ("the app", "we") handles information when you use the app. We built LUXE DASH to work offline, and we designed it to collect as little information as possible.</p>

<h2>1. Information We Collect</h2>
<p><strong>We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to our servers. We do not operate any server that receives your data.</strong></p>
<p>The app does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, or phone number.</p>

<h2>2. Location Data (GPS)</h2>
<p>The app requests access to your device's location in order to:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Calculate your current speed for the speedometer</li>
  <li>Show your heading on the compass</li>
  <li>Calculate trip distance, duration, and related trip statistics</li>
  <li>Retrieve local weather information for your area</li>
</ul>
<p>Location data is processed <strong>on your device only</strong>. It is not stored on any server, is not shared with us, and is not sold or transferred to third parties. Trip statistics are kept locally on your device and are cleared when a new trip begins.</p>

<h2>3. Weather Data</h2>
<p>To display current weather, the app sends your approximate coordinates to <a href="https://open-meteo.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open-Meteo</a>, a third-party weather service. Only coordinates are sent — no identifiers, no account information. This is the only network request the app makes for content. If you have no internet connection, the app simply shows the last saved reading or hides the weather panel.</p>

<h2>4. Data Stored on Your Device</h2>
<p>The app saves your preferences locally on your device, including:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Your selected car logo and background theme</li>
  <li>Your interface color preferences</li>
  <li>Your most recent trip statistics and last weather reading</li>
  <li>Your purchase status</li>
</ul>
<p>This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.</p>

<h2>5. Purchases</h2>
<p>LUXE DASH offers a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks premium features permanently. All payments are processed by <strong>Google Play</strong>. We never see or store your payment card details, billing address, or any financial information. Please refer to <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Google's Privacy Policy</a> for how Google handles payment data.</p>

<h2>6. Permissions We Use</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Location (GPS)</strong> — for speed, compass, trip data, and weather</li>
  <li><strong>Internet</strong> — for weather updates and purchase verification only</li>
  <li><strong>Photos / Media</strong> — only if you choose a custom background from your gallery. The selected image is copied to the app's private storage and is never uploaded anywhere.</li>
</ul>

<h2>7. Analytics and Advertising</h2>
<p>The app contains <strong>no advertising</strong> and <strong>no third-party analytics or tracking</strong> tools. We do not track your behaviour, build profiles, or share information with advertisers.</p>

<h2>8. Children's Privacy</h2>
<p>LUXE DASH is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.</p>

<h2>9. Changes to This Policy</h2>
<p>We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.</p>
